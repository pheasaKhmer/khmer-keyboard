# Play Store listing (draft)

Text and answers for the Play Console. The Khmer should be checked by a native speaker
before publishing.

## Main store listing

**App name** (30 characters at most): Khmer Keyboard

**Short description** (80 at most):

> Type Khmer with Latin letters, the way you chat: sok sabay te → សុខសប្បាយទេ

**Full description**:

> Type Khmer the way you already do in chat, with Latin letters, and get Khmer script.
> Type "sok sabay te" and the keyboard writes សុខសប្បាយទេ.
>
> • Reads chat spellings: "sous dey", "suosdey" and "sursdey" all give សួស្តី, and common
>   abbreviations work too (nh, tv, hz, jg).
> • A suggestion bar like on the iPhone: what you typed on the left, the best reading in
>   the middle (space picks it), the next on the right, and more behind the arrow.
> • Context helps: after ការ, "bong" gives បង់.
> • Learns the words you pick, most of all after the same word.
> • A Khmer script layout (NiDA) for typing Khmer directly, a tap away.
> • Select Khmer text to see it in Latin letters, in chat style or UNGEGN.
>
> Private by design: the app has no internet access and no permissions. What you type and
> the words it learns stay on your phone.

**App category**: Tools

**Contact**: the GitHub issues page, https://github.com/pheasaKhmer/khmer-keyboard/issues

**Privacy policy**: https://github.com/pheasaKhmer/khmer-keyboard/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
