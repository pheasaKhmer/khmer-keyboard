# Privacy policy: Khmer Keyboard

Effective 5 October 2026.

Khmer Keyboard does not collect, send or share any of your data.

## No internet access

The app asks Android for no permissions at all, not even network access. It cannot send
anything off your phone, and it has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or other
third-party services.

## What you type

Your typing is read on the phone, to suggest Khmer readings, and is not stored, except for
the following:

- **Learned words.** When you tap a suggestion, the keyboard saves the letters you typed,
  the Khmer word you picked, the Khmer word before it, and how often you picked it, so the
  word ranks higher next time. They are kept in a file in the app's private storage, which
  other apps cannot read, and they are left out of Android backups. Nothing is learned in
  password, email, web address or number fields.
- **The layout you used last** (Khmer script or romanized), so the keyboard opens with it.

To delete learned words, open the app and tap **Forget learned words**. Uninstalling the
app deletes everything it stored.

When you turn on any keyboard that does not come with your phone, Android warns that it
may be able to collect what you type. This keyboard does not; the source code is public
at <https://github.com/pheasaKhmer/khmer-keyboard>.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new
date. Questions can be asked at <https://github.com/pheasaKhmer/khmer-keyboard/issues>.
